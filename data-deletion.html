Delete your Nationality Test by Face data

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This page explains how to delete data related to Nationality Test by Face, currently displayed as VibeCheck in the Android app (com.cukkacreatives.skinscanner), by Cukka Creatives. The app has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete: your scan history lives on your phone, and only the data needed to analyze your photos and run the app leaves it. You can delete most of it yourself, and you can ask us to delete the rest.

1. Delete the data on your phone

Your scan history, results, photo thumbnails, unlock state, daily scan counters, settings, privacy choices, ad-free promotion code status and Premium status are saved only in the app's private storage on your device.

  • Scan history for one tool: open that tool's History screen and tap Clear History. This removes the saved results of that tool from the app.
  • One result: reporting a result with Report this result also removes it and its thumbnail from your phone.
  • Everything on your phone: open your phone's Settings → Apps → VibeCheck → Storage and tap Clear storage (or Clear data), or uninstall the app.

This cannot be undone. The app turns off Android backup for its data, so no copy stays in your Google account backup. Share cards you saved to your gallery or sent to other apps are outside the app and are not removed. Deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel a Premium subscription; manage it in Google Play.

2. Ask us to delete your data

To ask us to delete data held on our servers:

  1. Email cukkacreatives@gmail.com with the subject "Nationality Test by Face Data Deletion Request".
  2. Because there are no accounts, tell us how to find your data: the approximate date and time, and the tool you used, of the scans, feedback messages or reports you want deleted. If you emailed us before, send your request from the same address.
  3. Do not send another face photo or an identity document. Do not send payment card details, passwords or other sensitive information.

We reply to confirm your request and delete the data within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Photos you submitted for analysis. These are deleted from our server automatically after processing, without a request.
  • Analysis requests and results held on our server, with their request data (tool, language, country setting, app version and app-specific device identifier).
  • Feedback messages you sent from the app, with the app version and app-specific identifier sent with them.
  • Content reports you sent with the Report button on a result screen, including the copy of the reported result, unless we must keep them as described below.
  • Analytics and crash data held by Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) on our behalf. It is tied to random app identifiers that we cannot link to you by name or email. Where we can identify your data, we delete it; otherwise it expires under Firebase's data retention rules.
  • Emails you sent us, including your deletion request, once the request is handled.

4. What is kept

  • Content reports about possibly illegal or harmful content may be kept as long as needed to review them, keep the service safe and meet legal obligations.
  • Information needed to prevent abuse or meet legal obligations may be kept as long as that need lasts.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play as long as tax, accounting and fraud-prevention laws require. We do not store them on a server of our own.

5. Data controlled by Google

Advertising data collected through Google AdMob, Google Play reviews and Google Play purchase records are controlled by Google. You can manage them yourself:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path depends on your phone).
  • Change your ad consent choices with Privacy settings in the app menu or Settings (shown in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). Your choice is stored on your phone and is removed when you clear the app's storage.
  • Cancel a Premium subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Edit or delete your review on the app's page in Google Play.
  • Manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.

More details are in our Privacy Policy.
